# Privacy Policy

Public page: `/privacy`. Last updated September 30, 2026.

FixTray operates fixtray.app. Members (the shop owner and that shop's employees, including technicians and managers) use FixTray at no charge. FixTray does not charge members a subscription.

A customer pays the shop's quote plus a FixTray service fee when that fee applies. The fee named in the product is a flat $5.00 per paid work order unless the platform has configured a different amount. The amount shown at checkout is the amount charged. Card payments are processed by Stripe. FixTray stores payment status, amount, and the Stripe payment identifier. A saved card stores brand, last four digits, expiration, and the Stripe payment-method identifier. FixTray does not store a full card number or a card security code.

Accounts store the name, email, username, and a bcrypt password hash, plus the shop and job records people enter. Location is stored when a technician shares it or a job has a map pin. A demo request stores the email address and sends a demo shop login from noreply@fixtray.app. That demo lasts 30 minutes starting at the first login, and then the password and demo shop changes are cleared.

Email is sent with Resend. Text messages, when used, go through Twilio. Photos go to Cloudinary. FixTray does not sell personal information.

Passwords are hashed with bcrypt. Sign-in uses an httpOnly session cookie, a refresh cookie, a security token on changes, rate limits, and role checks.

There is no self-serve account deletion button. Email support@fixtray.app.

A mobile app is being built and is not in the app stores yet. Until then, use the website, including a phone browser. On iPhone, use Safari and Add to Home Screen.
