# Terms of Service

Public page: `/terms`. Last updated September 30, 2026.

These terms cover use of fixtray.app. FixTray is a work-order system for auto service. A mobile app is being built and is not in the app stores yet. Until then, use the website, including a phone browser. On iPhone, use Safari and Add to Home Screen.

A member is a shop-side user: the shop owner and that shop's employees, including technicians and managers. FixTray is free for members. FixTray does not charge members a subscription and does not publish a member price.

A customer pays the shop's quote plus a FixTray service fee when that fee applies. The fee named in the product is a flat $5.00 per paid work order, unless the platform has configured a different amount. The amount shown at checkout is the amount charged. No fee is added when the quote is zero or the configured fee is zero. The shop receives the quote. FixTray retains the service fee. If the shop has not connected Stripe, FixTray does not charge that invoice.

A demo emails a login for a demo shop. It is not a meeting. The demo shop is only so someone can see if they like FixTray before signing up. No real work is to be done in it. It is not their shop. The demo lasts 30 minutes, starting at the first login, not when the email is sent and not when the form is submitted. When the 30 minutes end, the password resets and changes in the demo shop reset too.

A customer approves work by signing the estimate. Email support@fixtray.app with an account question.
